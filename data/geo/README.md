# India States GeoJSON Authority & Boundary Disclaimer

## Official Boundary Statement
The state boundaries in this dataset reflect the Survey of India demarcations of the Republic of India, including the Union Territories of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh, and the State of Arunachal Pradesh.

## Source & Provenance
- **Dataset:** Official Survey of India / Open Data India Administrative Boundaries
- **Format:** GeoJSON (EPSG:4326 - WGS84)
- **Features:** 28 States and 8 Union Territories with standard ISO-3166-2:IN state codes (`IN-AP`, `IN-AR`, `IN-AS`, `IN-BR`, etc.) and official names matching `config/states.yaml`.
- **License:** Open Government Data (OGD) License India / Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

## Cartographic Disclaimer
"The representation of state boundaries, internal administrative divisions, and names used on this map are for analytical and visualization purposes in THE SILENCE INDEX. Low record counts or absence of data for any region reflects the documentation completeness of the analysed institutional collections, not the historical depth, cultural vibrancy, or intrinsic value of that region's heritage."
