# THE SILENCE INDEX — Methodology & Mathematical Framework

> **Tagline:** *Because what is missing from history matters too.*  
> **Core Concept:** An intelligence platform for auditing cultural heritage collections to identify what is documented, partially documented, undocumented in the analysed collection, or impossible to assess.

---

## 1. Ethical & Epistemological Axioms

1. **Absence of Record is Not Absence of Heritage**  
   The absence of a record in a museum, library, or digital archive is never evidence that a community, tradition, or region lacks rich cultural expression. It reflects only the institutional priorities, cataloguing biases, colonial histories, or resource constraints of the collector. All diagnostic language in the platform explicitly states **"not documented in the analysed collection"**.

2. **Completeness vs. Value**  
   Cultural Coverage Debt (CCD) measures metadata completeness and descriptive thoroughness. It **never** ranks communities or cultural traditions by importance, artistic merit, or historical value.

3. **Consent Before Documentation**  
   Living traditions, sacred ceremonies, ritual objects, and indigenous knowledge systems belong to their custodian communities. Recommendations generated by the platform that touch practitioner perspectives, oral histories, or sensitive domains are strictly routed through **"Consultation Required"** protocols. The platform never generates Traditional Knowledge (TK) labels autonomously.

4. **Immutable Provenance**  
   Original metadata provided by institutions or users is never silently overwritten. Normalized or inferred values are tagged with explicit provenance badges (`SOURCE`, `NORMALISED`, `INFERRED`), confidence scores, and processing timestamps.

---

## 2. The Four-State Absence Model

Every record $r$ is evaluated across each applicable documentation dimension $d$ into one of four mutually exclusive states (or flagged as not applicable):

| Status | Meaning | Operational Criteria | Visual Pattern |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Documented** | Field is mapped and data meets high-confidence standard | $\text{Score}(r, d) \ge 0.75$ | Solid Olive Fill (`#3F4220`) |
| **Partial** | Some metadata exists but lacks depth or provenance | $0.25 \le \text{Score}(r, d) < 0.75$ | Antique-Gold Diagonal Stripes (`#C8A24A`) |
| **Undocumented** | Column exists in dataset, but record value is null or empty | $\text{Score}(r, d) < 0.25$ | Copper Hatch Pattern (`#B0673A`) |
| **Not Assessable** | Dataset contains no column capable of recording this dimension | Missing schema mapping | Neutral Parchment Dotted Border (`#D9C8A4`) |
| **Not Applicable** | Dimension is structurally irrelevant for this category | Configured in `applicability.yaml` | Neutral Dash (`—`) |

## 3. The Ten Documentation Dimensions & Deterministic Rubrics

Each record receives a score $S(r, d) \in [0.0, 1.0]$ determined strictly by deterministic rules (defined in `config/rubrics.yaml`):

### 1. Visual Documentation (`visual_documentation`)
- `+0.60`: Direct, accessible image URL or digital media asset reference.
- `+0.20`: Image technical metadata present (resolution dimensions, file format, or alternate angle views).
- `+0.20`: Declared rights or licence statement present.

### 2. Historical Context (`historical_context`)
- `+0.40`: Dating, century, or dynastic period recorded.
- `+0.30`: Contextual narrative or historical description $\ge 200$ characters.
- `+0.30`: Scholarly citation, bibliographic source, or reference catalog cited.

### 3. Original-Language Documentation (`original_language`)
- `0.00`: English-only text or unclassified Latin script.
- `0.50`: Language tag present or transliterated vernacular title provided (IAST, ISO 15919).
- `1.00`: Description or title provided in indigenous Indic script (detected via Unicode blocks: Devanagari, Bengali, Gurmukhi, Gujarati, Odia, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, or Perso-Arabic).  
*Note: Labelled as a rule-based heuristic.*

### 4. Geographic Provenance (`geographic_provenance`)
- `+0.40`: Indian State or Union Territory identified.
- `+0.30`: District, town, or specific archaeological site identified.
- `+0.30`: Geographic coordinates or authority gazetteer identifier (e.g., GeoNames / Wikidata P131).  
*Finding rule:* Contradictory geography (e.g., state labeled "Bihar" with district "Thanjavur") generates an explicit diagnostic alert rather than silent correction.

### 5. Materials and Techniques (`materials_techniques`)
- `+0.50`: Base material specified (e.g., bronze, terracotta, mulberry silk, palm leaf).
- `+0.50`: Specific fabrication technique specified (e.g., lost-wax casting, ikat resist dyeing, kalamkari, temple carving).

### 6. Practitioner or Artisan Perspective (`practitioner_perspective`)
- `+0.40`: Named artisan, master craftsman, or scribe.
- `+0.30`: Guild, lineage, gharana, caste community, or workshop noted.
- `+0.30`: Practitioner-authored or community-contributed oral statement included.

### 7. Oral Histories and Associated Narratives (`oral_histories`)
- `1.00`: Linked audio/video recording, oral history interview, or ethnographic recording.
- `0.50`: Recorded folklore, associated song lyrics, or local narrative tradition summary.

### 8. Source Attribution and Provenance (`source_attribution`)
- `+0.30`: Holding institution or source repository named.
- `+0.20`: Accession number or permanent catalog identifier.
- `+0.30`: External URI or published catalog link.
- `+0.20`: Provenance ownership chain recorded.

### 9. Community Validation (`community_validation`)
- *Assessability Gate:* Only assessable if the dataset includes fields such as `validated_by`, `consulted_with`, or `community_notes`. If absent, defaults to **Not Assessable** (never "Undocumented").
- `1.00`: Explicit community approval or custodian review documented.
- `0.50`: Collaborative consultation ongoing.

### 10. Preservation and Accessibility Information (`preservation_accessibility`)
- `+0.40`: Physical condition report or conservation history documented.
- `+0.30`: Open access rights or machine-readable reuse licence declared.
- `+0.30`: Public access URL or persistent repository identifier provided.

## 4. Cultural Coverage Debt (CCD)

### Mathematical Definition
For each dimension $d$, the collection coverage $C_d$ is computed as the mean score across all records where $d$ is both applicable and assessable:

$$C_d = \frac{1}{|R_d|} \sum_{r \in R_d} S(r, d)$$

The dimensional debt is the complement of coverage:

$$\text{Debt}_d = 1.0 - C_d$$

The overall **Cultural Coverage Debt (CCD)** is the weighted percentage of unmet documentation across all assessable and applicable dimensions:

$$\text{CCD} = 100 \times \frac{\sum_{d \in \mathcal{A}} w_d \cdot \text{Debt}_d}{\sum_{d \in \mathcal{A}} w_d}$$

Where $\mathcal{A}$ is the set of assessable dimensions and $w_d$ are the dimension weights (defaulting to equal weights $w_d = 1.0$).

### Uncertainty & Statistical Rigor
Because metadata samples can vary in size, every CCD score is reported alongside:
1. **Assessability Ratio:** e.g., *"CCD 33.9 on 7 of 10 dimensions assessable"*.
2. **Bootstrap 90% Confidence Interval:** Computed over 1,000 resamples with fixed random seed for full reproducibility:
   $$\text{CI}_{90} = [\text{Percentile}_{5}, \text{Percentile}_{95}]$$
3. **Small-Sample Safeguard:** For any slice with fewer than 5 records, numeric scores are suppressed and labelled **"Insufficient Evidence"** to prevent misleading statistical claims.

## 5. Preservation Priority Engine

Preservation documentation actions are surfaced by evaluating slice-dimension gaps $(S, d)$:

$$\text{Priority} = 100 \times \left(0.40 \cdot G + 0.25 \cdot E + 0.20 \cdot B + 0.15 \cdot F\right)$$

Where:
- **$G$ (Gap Magnitude):** The dimensional debt of slice $S$ on dimension $d$.
- **$E$ (Evidence Confidence):** Function of slice sample size, data completeness, and classification certainty.
- **$B$ (Breadth):** Proportion of the total collection affected by this gap ($|S| / |R|$).
- **$F$ (Feasibility):** Pragmatic score evaluating whether leads already exist in the metadata (source URLs, accession links) and distinguishing desk research from field expeditions.

Priority bands are labelled strictly as:
- **High Investigation Priority** ($\ge 70$)
- **Medium Investigation Priority** ($45 - 69$)
- **Lower Investigation Priority** ($< 45$)
